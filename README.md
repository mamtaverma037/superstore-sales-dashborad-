# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Superstore Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales, profit, orders, products, customers, and regional performance.

The dashboard transforms raw Superstore sales data into an interactive management report using **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Timeline, KPIs, and Excel formulas**.

The main goal of the project is to understand sales performance and identify important business trends and patterns.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Track key business KPIs
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Analyze regional and segment-wise performance
* Understand monthly and yearly sales trends
* Identify profitable and loss-making areas
* Create an interactive dashboard for management reporting

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales & Profit Trend

Analyzed sales and profit over time to identify:

* Monthly sales trends
* Growth and decline periods
* Seasonal patterns
* Changes in profitability

### 2. Category Analysis

Compared the performance of major product categories such as:

* Furniture
* Office Supplies
* Technology

This helps identify which categories contribute the most to sales and profit.

### 3. Sub-Category Analysis

Analyzed sub-categories to identify:

* High-performing products
* Low-performing products
* High-profit sub-categories
* Areas with lower profitability

### 4. Regional Analysis

Compared sales and profit across different regions to understand geographical performance.

### 5. Segment Analysis

Analyzed customer segments such as:

* Consumer
* Corporate
* Home Office

This helps understand which customer segments contribute most to overall business performance.

### 6. Product Analysis

Identified top-performing products based on sales and profit and analyzed products with weaker profitability.
